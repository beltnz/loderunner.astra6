About this edition & credits

This local edition runs the reconstructed 1983 Apple II 6502 program. Gameplay, maps, animation and scoring come from the original program; keyboard, disk access, display and audio are browser adapters. Artifact colors approximate an Apple II color monitor. This is an unofficial preservation project, not affiliated with the rights holders.

THE RULES OF THE UNDERGROUND

Collect. Dig. Escape.

Collect every gold chest to reveal the escape ladders, then reach the top of the room. Lure guards into holes, run over trapped guards, and keep moving before the bricks close.

Move
Arrow keys or original I / J / K / L. Movement continues until you press Space or another direction.
Dig
Z / X, or original U / O. Dig the brick diagonally below you. You need a clear space beside you.
Watch your step
You can climb ladders and hang from ropes. Some bricks are trap doors. Guards can carry gold; trap them to recover it.
Scoring
250 per gold chest, 75 for trapping a guard, 75 for burying one, and 1,500 plus an extra life for escaping a room. Start with five lives.
Stuck?
Ctrl+A sacrifices a life and restarts the room. Escape pauses. New game resets the run. Practice starts a fresh run at the selected room.
Keep your progress
Save game stores a snapshot in this browser. Export a save for a portable backup. Browser storage availability depends on your browser settings.
